Watched Together

Adds a shelf to the home screen showing what other people on this server have watched recently, with their profile pictures on each card. Requires the Home Screen Sections, File Transformation and Plugin Pages plugins. After installing, enable the "Watched Together" section in Home Screen Sections' settings.

Shelf

Shown above the row on everyone's home screen.
Only plays within this many days appear.

What counts

Who watched it

Pictures scale with the card, so they stay in proportion on phones, laptops and TVs. 16 is the default; try 22–28 for bigger faces.
Updates every 15 seconds while the home screen is open.

Watch together

They choose "Ask to watch together" or "Watch on my own". Asking sends the person watching an invite; nothing happens unless they accept. If they're already in a SyncPlay group, you join it directly. Invites appear in the web client and web-based apps, and work with a mouse, touch, keyboard, TV remote or gamepad.

Most Popular shelves

Ranked by total watch time from the Playback Reporting plugin, which must be installed. After changing titles or switching a shelf on, enable it in Home Screen Sections' Section Settings.

1st, 2nd and 3rd get gold, silver and bronze medals; the rest show their rank number.

Privacy

Ticked users' viewing is never shown to anyone. Ticked libraries never appear on the shelf.
